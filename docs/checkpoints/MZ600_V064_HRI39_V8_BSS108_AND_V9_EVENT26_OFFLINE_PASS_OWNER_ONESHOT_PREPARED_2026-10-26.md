## Source-grounded legacy layout and event

[Original Broadcom 2010 `wlioctl.h`](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/hardware/broadcom/wlan/+/froyo/bcm4329/src/include/wlioctl.h) declares BSS **version108**; [later Broadcom 109 header](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/hardware/broadcom/wlan/+/b5876d709a5573570d2ee218a3fabce6fc18d0db/bcmdhd/dhdutil/include/wlioctl.h) confirms 109 adds the trailing SNR while v108 ends immediately after the IE length at byte124. Current pinned Linux 7 `brcmf_bss_info_le` has matching offsets: SSID_len 18, ctl_ch 88, IE offset 116, IE length 120, SNR 124. `BUILD_BUG_ON` asserts offsets so a rebase fails at compile time rather than decoding arbitrary memory. Boundaries/IE TLVs must be validated independently of record envelope lengths. Do not globally downgrade Linux's v109 parser.

Linux7 `brcmfmac/fweh.h` defines **BRCMF_E_SCAN_COMPLETE=26** and modern **BRCMF_E_ESCAN_RESULT=69**. Existing `brcmf_init_escan` registers only event69, not event26. A new event26 handler can reveal whether HRI39 firmware emits it without modifying cfg80211 scan lifetime. Its existence in headers does NOT prove the device emits it.
