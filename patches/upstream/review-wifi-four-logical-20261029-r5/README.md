## Source-verified regulatory order

The pinned kernel's `net/wireless/core.c` stores each channel's `orig_flags` when `wiphy_register()` runs. The pinned `brcmf_cfg80211_attach()` calls `wiphy_apply_custom_regulatory(wiphy, &brcmf_regdom)` and `wiphy_register()` **before** `brcmf_setup_wiphybands(cfg)`, which invokes WIFI-01's legacy firmware query. **Do not write current firmware country-limited channels into `orig_flags`: that field represents original hardware/board constraints.** [Linux Wireless primary regulatory design](https://wireless.docs.kernel.org/en/latest/en/developers/regulatory/processing_rules.html).

For *accepted* USER or COUNTRY_IE regulatory updates, `net/wireless/reg.c:wiphy_update_regulatory()` calls `handle_band()` and rebuilds channel flags from `orig_flags` and applicable regulatory rules **before** `reg_call_notifier()`; our stock `brcmf_cfg80211_reg_notifier()` sets the requested firmware country when valid and reruns `brcmf_setup_wiphybands()`. Thus a country-dependent `DISABLED` flag added by WIFI-01 is **not unconditionally permanently frozen**: cfg80211 may clear it at the next accepted update when permitted by original flags and the then-current rules. But **not every notification is such an update**: custom-regulatory `CORE` hints can call the notifier without a preceding channel recomputation. In addition, firmware country updates can fail or be skipped (e.g., same country abbreviation), so this ordering cannot be treated as a universal proof of fresh or legally sufficient channel state.

The default Broadcom `brcmf_regdom` is `alpha2="99"` with wide 5GHz channel rules that **do not include per-channel DFS or NO_IR bits**. The code `reg_get_regdomain()` generally chooses the PHY's custom domain rather than global rules, except in USER/COUNTRY_IE cases. Consequently, a global `iw reg get` country such as US is not sufficient proof that the per-phy custom domain has country-appropriate radar/passive restrictions. This is why we **must NOT remove the provisional broad 5GHz `NO_IR`** until an equivalent, trustworthy per-channel restriction source is available and verified.

**Most analogous Broadcom vendor precedent:** the [`bcmdhd` `wl_construct_reginfo` implementation](https://android.googlesource.com/kernel/msm/+/android-wear-5.1.1_r0.10/drivers/net/wireless/bcmdhd/wl_cfg80211.c) recognizes native `BCME_UNSUPPORTED` for `per_chan_info`, skips further unsupported calls, and takes an existing firmware channel inventory rather than inventing metadata. That behavior **does NOT by itself establish safe DFS/active 5GHz operation** under our different pinned `brcmfmac` `99` domain and firmware. [Broadcom historical ioctl definitions](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/hardware/broadcom/wlan/+/492884be0b302c6c6ea51b360c1f7b0996d96a8b/bcm4329/src/include/wlioctl.h) provide `WLC_GET_VALID_CHANNELS` and `WLC_GET_CHANNELS_IN_COUNTRY` for *channel enumeration*, not verified radar/passive policy information.

## Exactly what r5 adds within WIFI-01

The prior r4 already replaced ambiguous `-EBADE` decisions with a **per-call native BCME result**, constrained to BCM4329 and made under the normal FIL protocol mutex (not toggling the shared `ifp->fwil_fwerr` flag). R5 maintains that approach and:

1. Skips interpreting channels with `orig_flags & IEEE80211_CHAN_DISABLED`, leaving permanent registration-time hardware/custom-domain exclusions intact.
2. If `per_chan_info` fails with a **genuine native -23**, still treats the command as unsupported and conservatively keeps 5GHz `NO_IR`. If it fails for another reason, **returns the firmware/transport failure** instead of treating it as harmless missing metadata. That aborts the current legacy reconstruction rather than continuing with unverifiable channel detail; failure after prior channel changes is **not transactional** and the notifier still has a `void` signature, so this is **not complete refresh atomicity**.
3. Documents why unknown 5GHz restrictions are kept passive pending proof. **No new country hint, no new firmware protocol, no bogus IEEE80211_CHAN_RADAR, no changed cfg80211 regulator, and no relaxation of restrictions.**

WIFI-02/03/04 remain logically separate. They were replayed from the old r4 files with unchanged source hunks; export metadata hashes naturally change with amended WIFI-01 ancestor. Storage/DT/ATAG upstream review patches remain separate.
