## Interpretation, limits, next steps
A reduced SDMMC2 host clock does not, **by itself**, cure F2 readiness in this test environment. Combining the **complete Motorola six-field override** with lower bus speed also does not cure it.

This does **not** exclude power/32k source, firmware boot sequencing, matching per-device firmware/NVRAM, host electrical characteristics, startup interrupts or chip reset as contributing causes. Driver readback confirms written firmware bytes, **not reliable firmware CPU execution**. Probe firmware CPU/SDPCM handoff next, then compare with OEM working behavior and original per-unit calibration. Do not hard-code MAC or regulatory info.

v015 remains the accepted temporary diagnostic platform; v018/v019 are controlled, **failed Wi-Fi experiments**, not permanent fixes.
